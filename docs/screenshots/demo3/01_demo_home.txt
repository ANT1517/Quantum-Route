QuantumRoute

QuantumRoute plans delivery-fleet routes on Hyderabad's road network with QPSO-noQUBO (tuned), a quantum-inspired swarm optimizer that runs on ordinary CPUs. It weighs travel time, distance, congestion delay and CO₂, can plan the fleet with marginal cost so its own traffic is spread out, re-plans around incidents and has an optional QUBO slot for route ordering. Benchmark Studio shows how it compares with PSO, GA, SA and Google OR-Tools (industry reference) under equal conditions — including where it does not win.
